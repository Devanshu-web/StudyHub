Submission Guidelines

Follow these guidelines to ensure your content gets approved quickly

1

Original Content Only

Submit only your original work. Plagiarized content will be rejected immediately.

2

Clear and Legible

Ensure your notes are neatly written, clear, and easy to read. Use good quality images or PDFs.

3

Accurate Information

Double-check facts, formulas, and solutions for accuracy before submitting.

4

Proper Formatting

Organize your content with clear headings, bullet points, and proper spacing.

5

Complete Chapters

Submit complete chapter notes or solutions rather than partial content.
