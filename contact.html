Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about StudyHub

How do I reset my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page. We'll send a password reset link to your registered email address.

Is StudyHub really free?

Yes! All basic features are completely free. We believe education should be accessible to every student.

Which classes are supported?

We currently support Classes 6 to 10 with NCERT syllabus for Mathematics, Science, and English.

How do I report a bug or issue?

Use the contact form above or email bugs@studyhub.in. Please include screenshots if possible.
